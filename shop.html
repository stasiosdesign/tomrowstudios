FAQ

Everything you need to know about access, delivery and support.

How do I access my purchase?

Once you've completed your purchase, you'll receive an email with a direct link to your course or digital product. Access is immediate and remains available indefinitely. You can log in anytime to download files or revisit materials.

Can I download the files?

Yes. All digital products and course materials are available for download. You own what you purchase and can store files locally or in your preferred cloud service.

What if I need a refund?

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee if you're not satisfied. Contact us with your order details and we'll process it promptly. No questions asked.

Are there group discounts?

For teams or studios purchasing multiple licenses, we offer custom pricing. Reach out through the Partners page to discuss your needs and we'll find a solution.

Do you offer certificates?

Course completions come with a digital certificate of achievement. This is yours to share, print, or add to your professional profile as you see fit.
